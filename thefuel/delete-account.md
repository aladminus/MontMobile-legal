# Delete your TheFuel account

**TheFuel** by **Mont Mobile** · Last updated: October 31, 2026

This page explains how to delete your TheFuel account and the data linked to it, and what happens to that data.

---

## 1. Delete your account in the app

1. Open **TheFuel** and sign in.
2. Go to **Settings → Delete account**.
3. Confirm the deletion.

You are signed out and your account is scheduled for deletion. If you change your mind, sign in again within 30 days and choose to restore your account.

If you own a vehicle that you share with other people, the app asks you to remove them from that vehicle first, so their entries are not deleted without warning.

## 2. Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, email [montmobile.legal@gmail.com](mailto:montmobile.legal@gmail.com) with the subject line **"Delete my account"**. Send it from the email address you use to sign in to TheFuel, or include that address in the message. We will confirm by email once your account is scheduled for deletion.

---

## 3. What is deleted

Within 30 days of your request, we permanently delete from our servers:

- your account (email address and display name);
- your vehicles and everything recorded for them: fuel and charging entries, odometer readings, car events, notes, costs and fuel station locations;
- your settings, saved charging locations and tariffs;
- trips you saved in the Trip Planner.

## 4. What is kept

- **Purchase records:** if you bought Pro access, Google Play, the Apple App Store and RevenueCat keep the transaction record as required for billing and tax purposes. We do not store payment card details.
- **Crash reports and optional usage analytics:** these are kept by Sentry and PostHog for a limited time and then deleted automatically under their retention periods. Analytics events are linked to a random device identifier, not to your account.
- **Data on your phone:** data stored inside the app on your device stays there until you uninstall the app or clear its data. Receipt photos you chose to save to your photo gallery stay in your gallery.

---

## 5. Delete some data without deleting your account

You can delete individual entries, trips and vehicles at any time in the app. You can also email [montmobile.legal@gmail.com](mailto:montmobile.legal@gmail.com) to ask us to delete specific data.
