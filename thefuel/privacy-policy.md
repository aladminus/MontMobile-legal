# Privacy Policy

**TheFuel** · Last updated: September 23, 2026

TheFuel ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and your rights.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

- **Account information:** the email address used to create your account. If you sign in with Google, we also receive the name associated with your Google account. A display name (your Google name, or the part of your email address before the "@") is shown to people you share a vehicle with.
- **Vehicle and fuel data:** vehicle details, fuel and charging entries, odometer readings, car events, notes, and costs you record in the app. This data is stored on our servers and synced across your devices.
- **Fuel station location:** when you log a fill-up, the location of the fuel station you selected is saved with that entry and synced to our servers. We do not track your location continuously or in the background.
- **Device location:** your approximate location is used on your device to show nearby fuel stations. It is not sent to our servers for this purpose.
- **Trip Planner:** when you plan a route, the start and destination you enter (place names and coordinates) are sent to our routing provider to calculate the route. Trips you choose to save are stored on our servers with your account. City searches that can't be matched on your device are sent to the same provider.
- **Receipt scanning:** receipt photos are processed on your device and are not uploaded. If the on-device reader cannot find the fuel values, the text read from the receipt (never the photo itself) may be sent to an AI provider to extract the amounts.
- **Usage analytics (optional):** only if you turn on "Share anonymous usage analytics" in Settings, the app sends a small set of usage events (for example "app opened" or "fuel entry saved") linked to a random device identifier, not to your account. These events never include your location, amounts spent, or notes. Analytics is off until you agree, and you can turn it off at any time.
- **Purchase information:** whether you have purchased Pro access. Payments are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store via RevenueCat. We do not store payment card details.
- **Crash logs and device information:** used to diagnose errors and improve stability, processed by Sentry.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

- To provide, sync, and back up your data across your devices.
- To authenticate your account securely.
- To show nearby fuel stations using your device location.
- To calculate routes and fuel costs in the Trip Planner.
- To read fuel receipts you scan.
- To let people you invite view and add entries for a shared vehicle.
- To process and verify Pro purchases.
- To diagnose crashes and improve app stability.
- If you opt in, to understand how the app is used so we can improve it.

We process your data to provide the service you signed up for, to keep it secure and working, and, for optional usage analytics, based on your consent.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate TheFuel. Each processes data only as needed for its role:

- **Supabase** — database, authentication, and server functions. Data is stored on servers in the EU/US. [Supabase Privacy Policy](https://supabase.com/privacy)
- **Google Sign-In** — optional sign-in with your Google account. [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **HeiGIT (openrouteservice)** — route calculation and place search for the Trip Planner; receives start and destination locations. [openrouteservice Privacy Policy](https://openrouteservice.org/privacy-policy/)
- **Google Maps** — displays route maps in the Trip Planner. [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **Anthropic** — extracts fuel amounts from receipt text when on-device scanning can't; receives receipt text only, never photos. [Anthropic Privacy Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy)
- **PostHog** — optional usage analytics, only with your consent; hosted in the EU. [PostHog Privacy Policy](https://posthog.com/privacy)
- **RevenueCat** — in-app purchase management. [RevenueCat Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy)
- **Sentry** — crash reporting and error monitoring. [Sentry Privacy Policy](https://sentry.io/privacy/)
- **Google Play / Apple App Store** — app distribution and billing. [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) · [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

We do not sell your personal data to any third party.

## 4. Shared Vehicles

If you share a vehicle, or join a vehicle someone else shares with you, every member of that vehicle can see its entries (fuel, costs, odometer, events, and notes) and the display name of the person who added each one. You can leave a shared vehicle, or as its owner remove members, at any time from within the app.

---

## 5. Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, all associated data is permanently deleted within 30 days.

---

## 6. Security

All data is encrypted in transit using TLS. Data stored on your device is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot access, and is protected by your device's built-in storage encryption. Authentication is handled by Supabase Auth with industry-standard security practices.

---

## 7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

- Access the data we hold about you.
- Request correction of inaccurate data.
- Request deletion of your account and all associated data.
- Export your data at any time from within the app.
- Withdraw your consent to usage analytics at any time in Settings.

## 8. Account and Data Deletion
